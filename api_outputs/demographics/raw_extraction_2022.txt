APLICATIONS AND APOINTMENTS
For the term begining in 2022, BPO received 828 aplications, including for
new apointments and reapointments. The Borough President apointed
449 aplicants, including 182 first-time apointments (40.5 percent) and 267
reapointments (59 percent). BPO rescinded membership from 25 people,
primarily for non-atendance.
After this round of apointments, there were 43 remaining vacancies acros the
Broklyn boards. This is due to two factors: first, the number of aplications
varied widely from board-to-board, with seven boards receiving les than 25
aplications total; and second, BPO encouraged Councilmembers to select
members who live or work in their Council districts, and in some cases the
geographic overlap betwen comunity district and Council district was
so smal that there were no aplicants from that area. Note that the number
of vacancies is constantly changing because membership is dynamic due to
resignations and removals.

AGE
The median age of Broklynites is 34.7, yet comunity board members
have historicaly tended to be older, and this year was no exception. Most
aplicants and apointes were age 45-64. Yet almost 23 percent of Broklyn
residents are 18 years old or younger, as such, Borough President Reynoso
is comited in the long-term to apointing two persons aged 16-18 to each
comunity board. Ading more young membership was a chalenge in 2022
as the BPO only received 17 total aplications from persons 24 and under.
Note, due to the updated aplication form, next year’s data wil alow for a
more precise age breakdown.

RACE AND ETHNICITY
In Broklyn, about 35.4 percent of the population identifies as White,
26.7 percent as Black or African American, 13.6 percent as Asian, and 5.5
percent fal outside of these categories, including those chosing two or
more races. Nineten percent identify their ethnicity as Hispanic or Latino.
The race and ethnicity categories provided on the comunity board
aplications do not corelate directly with those provided on the Census,
so a direct comparison is not posible. Aditionaly, aproximately 27
percent of aplicants did not answer this question, and respondents who
did were able to chose multiple categories, meaning the numbers do not
corelate directly with the total number of apointes. The data colected
shows the largest number of apointes said they identified as White/
European or African American/Black, folowed by Latino/Hispanic. Only
about 7 percent of apointes identified as Asian.

LOKING FORWARD
BPO’s goal for future apointment rounds is to recruit a diverse group
of aplicants who acurately reflect Broklyn’s residents, as wel as a
suficient number of those aplicants to fil al 900 seats.
BPO is developing a robust, targeted outreach and engagement plan
using traditional and non-traditional tols to bolster citizen awarenes of
comunity boards and increase the number of aplicants. This includes
but is not limited to leveraging relationships with comunity and resident
organizations, interest groups, elected oficials, and schols and coleges to
expand outreach.
To facilitate geographic diversity acros comunity districts, BPO wil
also map the adreses of this cycle’s aplicants, apointes, and board
members to identify the geographic areas that require hyperlocal outreach.
The aplication form wil also be updated to colect more granular data.
