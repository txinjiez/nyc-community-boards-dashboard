New York City continues to invest in its extensive multi-modal transportation network, which alows its
residents to drive, walk, bike, use public transportation or any modal combination to comute for work
or leisure. Our comunity board members represent every user type. Th e analysis of al board members
shows that almost every member uses more than one transportation option.
A vast majority of members rely on public transportation for most of their comuting neds. First-time
apointes (33%) travel frequently by bus, and 55% comute regularly on the subway system. Among al
members, Comunity Board 2 has the highest usage of public transportation (80%); folowed by CB 9
(74%), the next closest is CB 7 (61%). An average 8% of al members always avoid public transportation.
Districts with the highest public transportation usage also lead the pedestrian category CB 2 (60%) and
CB 9 (52%). CBs 6, 10 and 15 are also boards with a high percentage of frequent walkers (50%). Broklyn-
wide, 41% of members walk as a primary transportation modality.
